Your Next Objective...

Begin Operation


When playing through Your Next Objective, you will be given a blurb of plot and you will be prompted to give a command.

The command is a text box where you can type whatever you want, but not every command will work.

You will have some context clues and a clear setting to help you make your commands.

Each prompt has a minimum of 2 paths that you can go down depending on your entry, so try some new stuff each run.

And if you're really lost as to what to type, a hint may be provided (if we can make it work!)
